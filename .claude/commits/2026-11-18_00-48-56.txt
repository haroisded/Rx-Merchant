3.2 By build step
  POS-B1  POS Opening Entry: Opening Amount per enabled mode, named
          POS-OPE-.YYYY.-, docstatus 1, status Open; a second Open session
          is refused. The register shows it until a session is open (an
          AlertDialog on phones, a centred card on tablets). When the last
          session was closed and nothing newer exists, "Cancel POS-CLO-..."
          reopens it.
  POS-B2  Totals engine (logic/totals.dart), in ERPNext's taxes_and_totals
          order: item amounts; included taxes worked back; tax rows (Actual
          shared by net amount, On Net Total, previous row amount / total,
          On Item Quantity) with item overrides and Not Applicable; the
          additional discount on Net Total or Grand Total (fixed charges
          excluded) spread over the lines; remainders on the last tax row;
          Rounded Total by the Smallest Currency Fraction Value; In Words
          (core/numbers/in_words.dart, Frappe's wording). Every worked
          example of the plan is a test.
  POS-B3  Item selector: sales items only (enabled, not templates, not past
          End of Life) with price and stock in the sale's warehouse; search
          by code, name, barcode; group filter with subgroups; grid of cards
          or a list (Hide images); Hide unavailable Items; Automatically Add
          Filtered Item To Cart; scan button; an exact barcode adds in its
          unit. Equal lines merge; a short stock item is refused with "Item
          is unavailable". The sale copies its defaults when it starts
          (POS-R2a).
  POS-B4  Item detail: quantity stepper, UOM (price looked up again), Rate,
          Discount (%), Discount Amount, Price List Rate, each read-only
          unless POS Settings / Selling Settings allow it; Max Discount
          refused as typed; Available Qty and Warehouse. Add Discount
          (percentage or amount). Number pad under the cart on landscape
          tablets.
  POS-B5  Payment: one card per enabled mode, number pad, Grand Total,
          Rounded Total, Paid Amount, To Be Paid or Change Amount; the
          default mode pre-filled (first Cash-type mode when the default is
          missing or disabled); split payments; a card or bank amount above
          what is due refused.
  POS-B6  Submit in one transaction: all POS-R16 checks reported together;
          name ACC-SINV-.YYYY.-; posting now and the current session; one
          ledger entry per line (per batch); each line's cost
          (incoming_rate); serial numbers Delivered with warranty expiry;
          a missing Item Price inserted once; Paid. Order summary with New
          Order.
  POS-B7  Save as Draft (no name, no stock; Validate Stock on Save);
          Action on New Invoice (Always Ask / Save / Discard) when a held
          order replaces unsaved changes; a held order is repriced when
          reopened and joins the session it is completed in. Recent Orders:
          Draft / Paid / Return filters, search by name, Edit and Delete
          for drafts.
  POS-B8  POS Closing Entry (/pos/closing): period, sales and returns,
          totals, one row per payment mode with Opening, Expected (opening
          + received - change, change charged to the sale's first cash
          payment), counted Closing Amount and Difference (error colour);
          Remarks; held orders count. Submit closes the session, then takes
          a backup (a failed backup is reported, the closing stands).
  POS-B9  A scanned serial number adds its unit; the line's Serial / Batch
          Entries in the item detail with Auto Fetch; empty lines picked at
          Submit (or refused, by the setting); only Available serials in the
          warehouse and unexpired batches can be sold.

      and number pad).

   9. Enter with one matching item added it although Automatically Add
      Filtered Item To Cart was off; removed.
   Standards
  10. The sale cost re-sync rounded in SQL, quantity totals were stored
      unrounded, some flt calls ignored the Rounding Method; all through
      flt with the method in force now.
  11. Number pad keys were about 44dp high on a phone; now at least 48dp.
  12. The In Words test moved to test/core/numbers/.
  13. The UOM menu could be built before unit names loaded (D-22); waits.
  14. Number fields now show "not a number" under the field.
  15. The merge rule moved from the cart notifier to logic/sale.dart
      (addToSale) with a test; Submitted typedef; pad -> numberPad,
      _line -> _lineRefusal.
  Found on the emulator and fixed:
  16. A number field wrote to its disposed controller when a side sheet
      closed (4 errors in logcat). Fixed; checked with a hot reload.
  17. The cancel-closing confirm had two "Cancel" buttons; the action now
      reads "Cancel POS-CLO-...".

  Left as they are, with the reason:

6. DECISIONS MADE ON YOUR BEHALF
--------------------------------

  1. No schema change. A held order gets its session and posting time
     re-stamped at Submit.
  2. The cart lives in memory (one Sale object in a Riverpod notifier);
     only Save as Draft persists it, which matches the plan's "offered on
     restart only if it was saved" (D-40).
  3. Complete Order and Open submit without a confirmation dialog (D-39).
  4. Rounding remainders (taxes included in the rate; a Grand Total
     discount) go to the last tax row, so Net Total + taxes = Grand Total
     (D-41).
  5. Change is charged to the sale's first Cash-type payment in the
     closing entry (D-43).
  6. A closed session is reopened by "Cancel POS-CLO-..." on the opening
     form; no list of closing entries (D-45).
  7. Medium side sheets open from the leading edge, over the selector
     (D-47); showRowEditor got a "leading" option.
  8. Tapping an empty payment mode fills the amount still due (D-48).
  9. A sale line's cost is re-synced when a backdated receipt recosts it
     (D-44).
 10. item_wise_tax_detail is keyed by sales_invoice_item id (D-42).
 11. In Words uses the international scale only (marked ponytail: in
     in_words.dart); the Indian lakh / crore are not written.
