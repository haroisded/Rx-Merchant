3.1 Decision you made (asked at the start)
  - Scope: all 13 build steps (B1 to B13), not only M2 and M3.

3.2 By build step
  STK-B1  Warehouse and Item Group as trees (nested ExpansionTiles, Add
          Child, flat-list switch), UOM and Brand.
          Rules: a warehouse with ledger entries cannot become a group
          (STK-R1); the Default Warehouse of Stock Settings stays enabled
          and non-group and cannot be deleted; renaming it renames it in
          Stock Settings (same pattern as the Default Price List, SET-R13).
          Whole-number units refuse fractions with ERPNext's message
          "Quantity cannot be a fraction at row 1" (STK-R3).
  STK-B2  Item list (search by code, name or barcode; scan button; filter
          chips Item Group, Disabled, Has Variants; thumbnail, stock dot,
          price) and Item form (strip with Stock Levels and Item Prices;
          tabs Details, Inventory, Variants, Sales, Tax). Item Code or
          Naming Series (STK-R4, with the Item Codification help text);
          Item Name pre-filled from the code; barcodes checked and owned by
          one item (STK-R8); units with conversion factors, locked once used
          on a document (STK-R9); re-order table; item tax table; image from
          camera or gallery at 512 px. On create, Standard Selling Rate
          becomes an Item Price and Opening Stock a submitted Material
          Receipt (Is Opening) (STK-R5). Maintain Stock, Has Batch No, Has
          Serial No, Valuation Method and Default UOM lock once the item has
          stock, with the reason shown (STK-R7).
  STK-B3  Item Price list and form (unit, batch, validity, note); overlapping
          validity refused; a template has no price. Pure price pick
          (STK-R10 steps 1-2) and priceFor with the default-list fallback
          (step 3); item tax pick and itemTaxTemplateOf up the group tree
          (STK-R13).
  STK-B4  Stock Settings page (SinglesForm, plus a new date field kind for
          Stock Frozen Upto). Setup > Stock now links to it. Defaults flow
          into new items. The Default Valuation Method locks once an item
          following it has stock (as ERPNext does).
  STK-B5  Engine (data/stock_ledger.dart, logic/valuation.dart): FIFO queue
          with ERPNext's negative-layer rules, Moving Average at 9 decimals,
          serial cost; each item-warehouse chain is replayed from its first
          entry; negative stock refused from the changed point on (batch and
          serial items never negative; no batch below zero); transfer-in
          rate = out rate + additional cost share, passed on to the target
          chain; Stock Frozen Upto; bin kept in step and rebuildable.
  STK-B6  Stock Entry: three purposes, Edit Posting Date and Time, scan
          field, row editor, additional costs spread by basic amount
          (STK-R39), Save / Submit / Cancel / Amend (name-1, -2), Stock
          Ledger link; opening stock from the item form.
  STK-B7  Stock Reconciliation: Opening Stock and Stock Reconciliation,
          current values as of the posting time, unchanged rows dropped,
          Fetch Items, Scan Mode, CSV download/upload (exact headers), batch
          and serial items refused with a pointer to Stock Entry.
  STK-B8  Backdated postings and cancellations recompute the chain or are
          refused with a message naming item, warehouse, quantity available,
          date and voucher.
  STK-B9  Reports: Stock Ledger, Stock Balance, Quick Stock Balance,
          Batch-wise Balance History, Serial No Ledger, Item Price Stock,
          Low Stock. Filter bar (a sheet on phones), totals row, CSV export,
          rows open their voucher. Low-stock badge on the Stock destination
          and the Low Stock link (switch: Show low-stock alerts).
  STK-B10 Item Attribute (text values with abbreviations, or a numeric
          range); templates; Create > Single Variant / Multiple Variants
          (codes such as TSHIRT-RED-S, existing combinations skipped);
          saving a template updates its variants, frozen fields skipped on
          stocked variants (STK-R14 to R17).
  STK-B11 Batches: master with derived status; batch on receipt (typed,
          or from the Batch Number Series); expiry mandatory for items with
          Has Expiry Date, defaulting to manufacturing date + shelf life;
          per-batch ledger; Material Issue may take expired batches,
          Transfer may not (STK-R18 to R22).
  STK-B12 Serial numbers: master (Inactive / Available / Delivered), series
          (PB2L.##### -> PB2L00001), status moved by stock documents only,
          each unit at its own cost, a receipt whose serials moved since
          cannot be cancelled (STK-R23 to R25, R32, R41).
  STK-B13 Inline Serial / Batch editor: add, edit, delete, Auto Fetch
          (FIFO / LIFO / Expiry), Scan (batch +1, serial twice refused),
          Create Serial Nos from Range (SN-01::10), CSV download/upload, 10
          per page, draft only; empty outgoing lines filled at submit when
          the setting is on (STK-R27).

   4. STK-R10 step 3 (default-list fallback) and STK-R13's group walk had

   5. STK-R19: expiry was neither mandatory nor defaulted for Has Expiry

      defaults it or refuses; the pickers list batch / serial items only.
      Test.
   6. A new Material Transfer started with source = target. The target is
      cleared when switching to Transfer.
   Standards
   7. Screens ran raw SQL. The queries moved to data/stock_lists.dart; the
      item strip became a provider (it re-subscribed on every rebuild).
   8. Submit was a TextButton; now a FilledButton (tonal, beside Save).
   9. Reconciliation quantity difference rounded at 9 decimals; now at
      Float Precision.
  10. A recost rewrote a Stock Entry's totals without setting modified_at;
      now set.
  11. The "posting time not edited" rule (posting = modified) was spelled
      out in four places; now one named function, postingSetByHand.
  12. Amended-name code existed twice; now one amendedName(table, id).
  13. core/widgets/adaptive_shell imported the stock feature and tested

  Found on the emulator and fixed earlier in the session:
  14. Default Unit of Measure blank on a new item: the form now waits for

  15. The report totals row crashed reports whose first column is a date

6. DECISIONS MADE ON YOUR BEHALF
--------------------------------

  1. No schema change. "Edit Posting Date and Time" has no column: a draft
     saved with it off stores the save time as both posting and modified
     time, so posting != modified means "set by hand"; Submit moves an
     unedited posting to the submit moment (postingSetByHand).
  2. A template's numeric range is narrowed by ticking values in Create >
     Multiple Variants (no extra columns on item_variant_attribute).
  3. Opening stock from the item form is a Material Receipt (Is Opening),
     as STK-R5 says, also for plain items (STK-R40 names reconciliation for
     the Opening Stock purpose; both work).
  4. Material Issue may take expired batches (the write-off path of
     STK-R22); Material Transfer may not.
  5. Every item-warehouse chain is replayed from its first entry (the
     plan's own stated ceiling, marked "ponytail:" in stock_ledger.dart).
  6. Reconciliation rows keep the differences computed at submit; later
     reposts do not rewrite them (the ledger is the truth).
  7. A batch created by Automatically Create New Batch without a series
     uses "<item code>-.#####".
  8. Price and tax lookups for the register (priceFor,
     itemTaxTemplateOf) were built now, so plan 02's rules are complete;
     the register calls them.
  9. Batch and serial FIFO: a batch by when it was created, a serial by
     when it came into the warehouse.
 10. ReportTable rewritten without DataTable (D-31).
