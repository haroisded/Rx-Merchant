6. DECISIONS MADE ON YOUR BEHALF
--------------------------------

  1. A return is never held as a draft; Submit saves and submits it in one
     transaction (D-61).
  2. A partial return refunds the original's additional discount and
     Actual (fixed) charges in proportion to the line amounts returned; a
     full return refunds exactly what was paid (D-62).
  3. The refund must equal the amount in full; no change on a refund;
     stored as negative payments (D-63).
  4. A return's cost is the original line's incoming_rate at the moment of
     the return; a later backdated recost does not follow (marked
     ponytail:, D-64).
  5. A batch line's return takes its quantity from the original's batches
     in the order they were sold (D-65).
  6. In Words keeps Frappe's wording ("Fifty-Four", the Fraction name as
     stored, "Cent"): INV-A2 is tested as "USD Seven Hundred And
     Fifty-Four and Ninety Cent only." (D-66).
  7. PDFs embed Roboto from the Flutter SDK (Apache 2.0); a symbol it
     lacks prints as the currency code (D-67).
  8. Raw text is printable ASCII; the barcode is the printer's own
     command (D-68).
  9. roundToFraction mirrors a negative amount (D-69).
 10. The print screen is a full-screen route over everything (openPrint),
     not a URL, so the register and the invoices open it alike (D-70).
 11. A draft in the invoice list opens in the register (/pos?heldOrder=)
     (D-71).
 12. The list's filters ride in the route's query; a session filter
     serves the closing entry's link (D-72).
 13. Renaming a Print Heading or Terms follows into POS Settings (D-73).
 14. Android settings are opened through a MethodChannel in
     MainActivity.kt, not a package (D-74).
 15. One Bluetooth connection per print job (ponytail:, D-75).
 16. Print Settings asks for the Nearby devices permission when it opens,
     since it lists the paired printers (D-76).
