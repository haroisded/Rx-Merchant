6. DECISIONS MADE ON YOUR BEHALF
--------------------------------

  1. The new page is called "POS Settings", ERPNext's own name for its
     POS settings doctype, and its keys are the POS Profile's field names
     (hide_images, tc_name, select_print_heading, ...).
     Cost: a reader may look for a "POS Profile" that is not there.
  2. The Default Price List guard replaces "at least one Price List stays
     enabled". It is stricter: the default list itself must stay enabled.
     Cost: to disable it you first pick another default in Selling
     Settings.
  3. Selling Settings keeps the default price list by name (as before), so
     a rename is carried across in the same transaction.
     Cost: one extra read on every price list save.
  4. Print Heading and Terms and Conditions are on POS Settings now, with a
     "None" choice, although their masters arrive with plan 05.
     Cost: two empty dropdowns until then.
  5. Unused messages and refusals were deleted, not kept for later
     (warehouse, itemGroups, taxesAndCharges, ...). Plan 02 or 03 adds them
     back when a screen needs them.
  6. The external plans folder was overwritten with the edited files (you
     asked for both copies). The two copies are identical again.
  7. Past records (both context-ai-response.txt files, the earlier session
     reports) were not edited.
