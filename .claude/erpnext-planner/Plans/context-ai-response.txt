Decisions I made that you may want to change:
- Invoice numbers are assigned on Submit, not on first save as ERPNext does, so discarded held orders leave no gaps in receipt numbers.
- A sale can be cancelled only while its POS session is open. After closing, the correction is a return.
- Batch and serial items are not handled by Stock Reconciliation. They are counted in and out through Stock Entry, following the older rule in the docs.
- A price-only credit note uses up the returnable quantity of that line, as ERPNext does.
