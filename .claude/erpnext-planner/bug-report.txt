Smaller issues
- "Add your first item" on the last Welcome step opens the Stock menu instead of the New Item form (grace, felipe, mehmet).
- Cut-off layout:
  - The item editor tab row cuts off "Tax" and "Variants" with no hint that it scrolls (8 personas).
  - The Dashboard "This Month" chip is cut off (4 personas).
  - The keyboard covers fields in the Welcome wizard and on New Item (4 personas).
- Accessibility: text fields have no name for screen readers (9 personas), and a few back buttons are 40dp, under the 48dp minimum.
- Unclear setup and wording:
  - The Welcome "Next" button is disabled with no hint why (malik).
  - The Valuation Rate defaults to 0, so the Dashboard shows Gross Profit 100% and Stock Value $0 (layla, malik, felipe).
  - Business jargon ("Material Receipt", "Valuation Rate", "UOM") loses new users (ravi, olga, anna).
